# What Are CloudOps Support Hours and Contact Options?

**Document Type:** FAQ
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** All Plans
**Tier:** Tier 1

## Summary

CloudOps offers different levels of support access depending on your subscription plan. This article explains how to reach support, what response times to expect, and which channels are available per plan.

## Support Channels by Plan

| Channel | Starter | Professional | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Help Center (self-service) | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Email ticket | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Live chat | ❌ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Phone support | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Dedicated CSM | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |
| Slack integration | ❌ | ❌ | ✅ |

## Response Time Targets

| Severity | Starter | Professional | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1 (service down) | 8 business hours | 2 hours | 30 minutes |
| P2 (major degradation) | 1 business day | 4 hours | 1 hour |
| P3 (minor issue) | 3 business days | 1 business day | 4 business hours |
| P4 (question) | 5 business days | 2 business days | 1 business day |

Response times are measured from ticket submission during business hours (9 AM–6 PM ET, Monday–Friday) for Starter plans. Professional and Enterprise 24/7 for P1/P2.

## How to Submit a Ticket

1. Visit [support.cloudops.example](https://support.cloudops.example).
2. Click **New Ticket**.
3. Select the severity level and describe your issue.
4. Attach relevant logs or screenshots.
