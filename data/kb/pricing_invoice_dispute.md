# Invoice Dispute Process

**Document Type:** Pricing
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** All Plans
**Tier:** Tier 1

## Summary

If you believe an invoice is incorrect — due to a billing error, unauthorized charge, or misapplied credit — this document explains how to dispute it and what to expect during resolution.

## Before Disputing: Self-Service Checks

Before filing a formal dispute, review these common explanations for unexpected charges:

1. **Usage overages:** Log in to [console.cloudops.example](https://console.cloudops.example) > **Settings > Billing > Usage** to see if storage or data transfer exceeded your plan's included allocation.
2. **Pro-rated upgrade charge:** If you upgraded mid-cycle, a pro-rated charge for the difference appears on your next invoice.
3. **Annual plan renewal:** Annual plans renew automatically. If you did not intend to renew, cancel per [policy_cancellation.md](policy_cancellation.md).

## Filing a Dispute

1. Log in to [console.cloudops.example](https://console.cloudops.example).
2. Navigate to **Settings > Billing > Invoices**.
3. Click the invoice in question.
4. Click **Dispute This Invoice**.
5. Select the dispute type:
   - **Incorrect amount**
   - **Duplicate charge**
   - **Service not received**
   - **Unauthorized charge**
   - **Other**
6. Add a description of the issue and any supporting details.
7. Click **Submit Dispute**.

You will receive a confirmation email with a dispute ticket ID.

## Resolution Timeline

- **Disputes under $500:** Resolved within 5 business days by Tier 1 billing support.
- **Disputes $500–$5,000:** Escalated to Tier 2 billing. Resolved within 10 business days.
- **Disputes over $5,000:** Reviewed by Finance. May take up to 20 business days.

## Dispute Outcomes

If the dispute is upheld:
- A credit is applied to your account for the disputed amount.
- The credit appears on your next invoice as **Billing Credit**.
- Refunds to the original payment method are only issued if the dispute involves a payment that failed to deliver any service.

If the dispute is rejected:
- You receive a detailed explanation.
- You may appeal by responding to the dispute ticket within 14 days.

## Chargebacks

Filing a credit card chargeback before completing the dispute process may result in account suspension pending review. Always attempt the dispute process first.
