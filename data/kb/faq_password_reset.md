# How Do I Reset My Password?

**Document Type:** FAQ
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** All Plans
**Tier:** Tier 1

## Summary

This article explains how to reset your CloudOps Platform password through the Web Console, including steps for standard email-based resets and recovery when you no longer have access to your registered email.

## Steps to Reset Your Password

1. Navigate to [console.cloudops.example/login](https://console.cloudops.example/login).
2. Click **Forgot Password?** below the sign-in button.
3. Enter your registered email address and click **Send Reset Link**.
4. Check your inbox for an email from `noreply@cloudops.example`. The link expires in **60 minutes**.
5. Click the link and enter a new password meeting the requirements: minimum 12 characters, at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one special character.
6. Click **Save Password**. You will be redirected to the login page.

## If You No Longer Have Access to Your Email

Contact Tier 1 support via the support portal. A support agent will verify your identity using your account's registered phone number or billing information before initiating a manual reset.

## Troubleshooting

- **Reset email not received:** Check your spam folder. If not found within 5 minutes, request a new link. Each account can request 3 reset emails per hour.
- **Link expired:** Return to the Forgot Password page and request a new link.
- **Error CO-401 after reset:** Clear your browser cache and cookies, then attempt to log in again.
