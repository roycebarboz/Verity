# What Happens During a Free Trial?

**Document Type:** FAQ
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** Starter (Trial)
**Tier:** Tier 1

## Summary

CloudOps Platform offers a 14-day free trial of the Professional plan, with no credit card required. This article explains what's included in the trial, any usage limits that apply, and what happens when the trial ends.

## Trial Details

- **Duration:** 14 days from signup
- **Plan level:** Professional feature set
- **Credit card required:** No
- **Auto-converts to:** Starter plan (unless you upgrade or enter billing info)

## What's Included in the Trial

During your trial, you have access to all Professional plan features:
- Up to 20 clusters
- Up to 25 team members
- Priority email and chat support
- Auto-scaling, multi-region deployments, and audit logs
- 99.9% uptime SLA (monitored, but not contractually enforced during trial)

## Trial Limits

Even during the Professional trial, the following limits apply:
- Maximum 5 node pools per cluster
- API rate limit: 100 requests per minute (Professional limit applies after conversion)
- No PagerDuty integration during trial

## What Happens When the Trial Ends

- **Day 14:** Trial expires. You are automatically downgraded to the **Starter plan** ($99/mo) if you have not entered billing information.
- **With billing info:** Your Starter subscription begins and you are charged $99 on day 15.
- **Without billing info:** Your clusters are suspended (not deleted) for 30 days. Enter billing info during this window to restore access.

## Upgrading During the Trial

You can upgrade to Professional or Enterprise at any time during the trial via **Settings > Billing > Subscription**.
