# How Do I Change My Subscription Plan?

**Document Type:** FAQ
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** All Plans
**Tier:** Tier 1

## Summary

You can upgrade or downgrade your CloudOps Platform subscription plan at any time via the Web Console. This article explains how plan changes work, when they take effect, and what billing adjustments to expect.

## Steps to Change Your Plan

1. Log in to [console.cloudops.example](https://console.cloudops.example).
2. Navigate to **Settings > Billing > Subscription**.
3. Click **Change Plan**.
4. Review the plan comparison and select your new plan.
5. Click **Confirm Change**.

## When Changes Take Effect

**Upgrades (e.g., Starter → Professional):** Take effect immediately. You are charged a pro-rated amount for the remainder of your billing cycle for the difference in plan cost.

**Downgrades (e.g., Professional → Starter):** Take effect at the start of your next billing cycle. You retain Professional features until then.

## Plan Limits

| Feature | Starter ($99/mo) | Professional ($499/mo) | Enterprise (custom) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clusters | 3 | 20 | Unlimited |
| Team members | 5 | 25 | Unlimited |
| Support | Email only | Priority email + chat | Dedicated CSM |
| SLA | 99.5% uptime | 99.9% uptime | 99.95% uptime |

## Upgrading to Enterprise

Enterprise plans require a custom pricing agreement. Click **Contact Sales** on the plan comparison page or email `sales@cloudops.example`.
