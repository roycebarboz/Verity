# Refund Policy

**Document Type:** Policy
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** All Plans
**Tier:** Tier 1

## Summary

This document describes CloudOps Inc.'s refund policy for all subscription plans, including the conditions under which refunds are granted, the refund calculation method, and how to request a refund.

## Monthly Plan Refunds

CloudOps does **not** issue refunds for monthly subscription charges once a billing cycle has begun. You retain access to your plan for the full period you paid for.

If you cancel your monthly plan, your access continues until the end of the current billing period and then stops. No pro-rated refund is issued.

## Annual Plan Refunds

Annual plan customers are eligible for a **pro-rated refund** for any unused full months remaining in their annual term, subject to the following conditions:

- The cancellation request must be submitted within 12 months of the annual subscription start date.
- Refunds are calculated as: `(months remaining) × (monthly equivalent rate)` where the monthly equivalent rate is the annual price divided by 12.
- Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days of approval.

**Example:** A Professional annual plan ($499/mo × 12 = $5,988/year) cancelled with 4 full months remaining would receive a refund of 4 × $499 = $1,996.

## Non-Refundable Charges

The following charges are non-refundable under any circumstance:
- Usage-based overage charges
- Setup or onboarding fees
- Add-on features (e.g., additional storage, premium support add-ons)
- Charges on accounts suspended for violation of the Acceptable Use Policy

## Exceptions and Discretionary Refunds

In extraordinary circumstances (e.g., extended platform outage exceeding SLA commitments), CloudOps may issue discretionary credits or refunds at management discretion. These are not guaranteed and are evaluated case-by-case.

SLA credits are governed separately — see [policy_sla.md](policy_sla.md).

## How to Request a Refund

1. Log in to [console.cloudops.example](https://console.cloudops.example).
2. Navigate to **Settings > Billing > Invoices**.
3. Click **Request Refund** on the relevant invoice.
4. Select the reason and submit.

Alternatively, contact Tier 1 support at [support.cloudops.example](https://support.cloudops.example).
