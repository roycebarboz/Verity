# Tax and VAT Handling

**Document Type:** Pricing
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** All Plans
**Tier:** Tier 1

## Summary

CloudOps Inc. is required to collect sales tax or VAT on subscription fees in certain jurisdictions. This document explains how tax is applied, how to provide a tax exemption certificate, and how to add a VAT registration number to your account.

## How Tax Is Calculated

Tax is calculated based on the **billing address** associated with your CloudOps account. Applicable rates are determined at the time of billing according to current tax law in your jurisdiction.

Tax is shown as a separate line item on your invoice, labeled **Sales Tax** (US) or **VAT** (EU/UK).

## US Sales Tax

CloudOps collects sales tax in US states where we have a tax nexus. If you are a tax-exempt organization (e.g., nonprofit, government entity), you can submit a tax exemption certificate:

1. Navigate to **Settings > Billing > Tax Exemption**.
2. Upload your exemption certificate (PDF or image).
3. Enter your exemption certificate number.
4. Click **Submit for Review**.

CloudOps reviews exemption certificates within 3 business days. Once approved, tax is removed from future invoices. Past invoices are **not** retroactively adjusted.

## European VAT

If you are a business based in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you may be eligible for reverse-charge VAT:

1. Navigate to **Settings > Billing > VAT Information**.
2. Enter your VAT registration number.
3. CloudOps validates the number against the VIES database (EU) or HMRC (UK).
4. Once validated, VAT is removed from your invoices and a note indicating reverse-charge applies is added.

If CloudOps does not yet collect VAT in your EU member state, your invoice may still show VAT applicable. Contact support if you believe there is an error.

## Invoices for Tax Purposes

CloudOps invoices include all information required for VAT reclaim purposes:
- CloudOps Inc. legal name and address
- Your billing name and address
- VAT number (where applicable)
- Tax amount broken out as a separate line item
