# How Do I Transfer Organization Ownership?

**Document Type:** FAQ
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** All Plans
**Tier:** Tier 1

## Summary

Organization ownership can be transferred to another team member by the current owner. This is typically done when a founder or admin leaves the organization. Only one person can hold the Owner role at a time.

## Prerequisites

- You must be the current **Organization Owner**.
- The recipient must already be a member of your CloudOps organization.

## Steps to Transfer Ownership

1. Log in to [console.cloudops.example](https://console.cloudops.example).
2. Navigate to **Settings > Team > Members**.
3. Locate the team member you wish to make the new owner.
4. Click the three-dot menu next to their name and select **Transfer Ownership**.
5. Review the confirmation dialog — this action is immediate and cannot be undone without the new owner's cooperation.
6. Click **Confirm Transfer**.

You will be automatically demoted to **Organization Admin** role. The new owner will receive an email confirmation.

## What the New Owner Can Do

The Organization Owner has all the same permissions as an Admin, plus the ability to:
- Delete the organization permanently
- Transfer ownership again
- Manage Enterprise contract terms (Enterprise plan only)

## If You Cannot Transfer (Owner Departed)

If the current owner is no longer reachable, contact Tier 1 support with the following:
- Proof of your authorization to manage the account (billing records, corporate email match)
- Written request for an administrative ownership transfer

These requests are reviewed by our Trust & Safety team within 2 business days.
