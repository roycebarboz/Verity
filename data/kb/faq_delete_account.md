# How Do I Delete My Account?

**Document Type:** FAQ
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** All Plans
**Tier:** Tier 1

## Summary

Account deletion is a permanent action that removes your CloudOps account, all associated clusters, and personal data according to our data retention schedule. This article explains how to request deletion and what happens to your data.

## Before You Delete

- **Export your data first.** See [faq_data_export.md](faq_data_export.md). Once deletion is confirmed, data cannot be recovered.
- **Cancel active subscriptions.** See [faq_cancel_subscription.md](faq_cancel_subscription.md). Outstanding invoices must be settled before deletion.
- **Transfer or remove team members.** If you are the Organization Owner, transfer ownership before deleting your personal account, or delete the entire organization.

## Steps to Request Account Deletion

1. Log in to [console.cloudops.example](https://console.cloudops.example).
2. Navigate to **Settings > Account > Danger Zone**.
3. Click **Delete Account**.
4. Enter your password to confirm.
5. Click **Permanently Delete Account**.

Your account will enter a **14-day grace period**. During this time, you can cancel the deletion by logging back in. After 14 days, all data is permanently deleted.

## Organization Deletion

To delete an entire organization (all members, clusters, and data):
1. Navigate to **Settings > Organization > Danger Zone**.
2. Click **Delete Organization**.
3. Type the organization name to confirm.

This action requires Organization Owner permissions.

## GDPR Deletion Requests

If you prefer to submit a formal GDPR right-to-erasure request rather than using the self-service flow, see [policy_privacy_summary.md](policy_privacy_summary.md).
