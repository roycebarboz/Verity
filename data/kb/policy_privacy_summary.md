# Privacy Policy Summary

**Document Type:** Policy
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** All Plans
**Tier:** Tier 1

## Summary

This document is a plain-language summary of CloudOps Inc.'s privacy practices. The full Privacy Policy is available at [cloudops.example/privacy](https://cloudops.example/privacy). This summary is intended to help support agents answer common customer questions.

## What Data CloudOps Collects

**Account Data:**
- Name, email address, company name
- Billing information (stored by our payment processor — we do not store raw card data)
- IP addresses used during login

**Usage Data:**
- Cluster configurations and deployment manifests you submit
- API usage logs and audit events
- Support tickets and communications

**Technical Data:**
- Browser type and version (for the Web Console)
- CLI version and operating system (anonymized telemetry, opt-out available)

## How CloudOps Uses Data

- To provide and operate the platform
- To send billing notifications and product updates
- To respond to support requests
- To detect fraud and enforce the Acceptable Use Policy
- To comply with legal obligations

## Data Sharing

CloudOps does **not** sell personal data to third parties. We share data only with:
- **Service providers** who operate on our behalf (cloud infrastructure, payment processing, email delivery) under data processing agreements
- **Law enforcement** when legally required (we notify affected customers where legally permitted)

## Customer Data (Your Workload Data)

Data stored within your clusters (databases, application data) is your data. CloudOps does not access, read, or process your workload data except:
- To provide support, with your explicit consent
- To respond to a valid legal order

## Your Rights

Under GDPR and applicable privacy laws, you have rights to access, correct, delete, and port your personal data. See [faq_gdpr_data_request.md](faq_gdpr_data_request.md).

See [policy_data_retention.md](policy_data_retention.md) for specific data retention periods.
