# Free Trial Terms

**Document Type:** Policy
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** Starter (Trial)
**Tier:** Tier 1

## Summary

This document describes the terms and conditions of the CloudOps Platform 14-day free trial, including what is included, limitations, and what happens when the trial ends.

## Trial Eligibility

- The free trial is available to new customers only. One trial per organization.
- No credit card is required to start the trial.
- Customers who have previously had a CloudOps account (including cancelled accounts) are not eligible for a new free trial.

## Trial Duration and Features

- **Duration:** 14 calendar days from account creation
- **Feature level:** Professional plan feature set
- **SLA:** Monitored but not contractually enforced during trial period

See [faq_free_trial.md](faq_free_trial.md) for full details on included features and limits.

## Trial Limitations

Even with Professional-level features, the following restrictions apply during the trial:
- Maximum 5 node pools per cluster
- API rate limit: 100 requests per minute
- No PagerDuty integration
- No dedicated SLA credit claims

## What Happens When the Trial Ends

**Option A — You add billing information:**
- If you add a payment method before day 14, you are automatically converted to the Starter plan ($99/mo) on day 15.
- You will not lose access to your clusters during conversion.

**Option B — You do not add billing information:**
- Your clusters are suspended (not deleted) at the end of day 14.
- Data is retained for 30 days from trial expiry.
- You can reactivate at any time during this 30-day window by entering billing information.
- After 30 days, all trial data is permanently deleted.

## No Automatic Charges

CloudOps will **not** charge you automatically at the end of the trial unless you have explicitly added a payment method and confirmed upgrade to a paid plan.

## Promotional Trials

CloudOps may occasionally offer extended trial periods through promotional campaigns. Promotional trials are subject to these terms plus any additional terms stated in the promotion.
