# Cancellation Policy

**Document Type:** Policy
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** All Plans
**Tier:** Tier 1

## Summary

This document describes the terms and process for cancelling a CloudOps Platform subscription, including what happens to your clusters and data after cancellation.

## How to Cancel

You may cancel your subscription at any time through the Web Console:

1. Log in to [console.cloudops.example](https://console.cloudops.example).
2. Navigate to **Settings > Billing > Subscription**.
3. Click **Cancel Subscription**.
4. Select a cancellation reason and click **Confirm Cancellation**.

For Enterprise customers, cancellations must be processed through your dedicated Account Manager. Self-service cancellation is not available for Enterprise accounts governed by a Master Service Agreement (MSA).

## What Happens After Cancellation

### During the Remaining Billing Period
Your subscription remains active until the end of your current billing period. All clusters, features, and support access continue normally.

### Post-Billing Period Grace Period (30 Days)
After your billing period ends:
- All clusters are **stopped** (not deleted).
- Data is retained for 30 days.
- You may reactivate your account at any time during this window by entering a new payment method and selecting a plan.

### After the Grace Period
- Clusters and all associated data are **permanently deleted**.
- Deletion is irreversible. Export your data before the grace period expires. See [faq_data_export.md](faq_data_export.md).

## Billing After Cancellation

- No further charges are made after cancellation.
- Any outstanding invoices must be paid before cancellation is finalized.
- Accounts with a CO-701 billing failure may not be cancelled until the outstanding balance is cleared.

## Reactivation

You may reactivate a cancelled account at any time during the 30-day grace period:
1. Log in to [console.cloudops.example](https://console.cloudops.example).
2. Navigate to **Settings > Billing**.
3. Click **Reactivate Account** and select a new plan.

After the grace period expires, reactivation creates a new account and does not recover deleted data.
