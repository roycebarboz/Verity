# Annual Discount Terms

**Document Type:** Pricing
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** Starter, Professional
**Tier:** Tier 1

## Summary

CloudOps Platform offers a 10% discount on Starter and Professional plans when you choose annual billing. This document explains how the annual discount works, payment terms, and how annual refunds are handled.

## Discount Details

| Plan | Monthly Price | Annual Price (10% off) | Effective Monthly Rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $99/month | $1,069.20/year | $89.10/month |
| Professional | $499/month | $5,389.20/year | $449.10/month |

The annual price is charged **upfront** for the full 12-month period at the time of subscription.

## How to Switch to Annual Billing

1. Log in to [console.cloudops.example](https://console.cloudops.example).
2. Navigate to **Settings > Billing > Subscription**.
3. Click **Switch to Annual Billing**.
4. Review the pro-rated amount due (if you are mid-cycle on monthly billing, the difference will be calculated).
5. Click **Confirm**.

The annual term begins on the day you switch.

## Auto-Renewal

Annual plans auto-renew at the end of the 12-month term at the then-current annual pricing. You will receive an email reminder **30 days before renewal**.

To disable auto-renewal:
1. Navigate to **Settings > Billing > Subscription**.
2. Click **Manage Renewal**.
3. Toggle off **Auto-Renew**.

With auto-renew disabled, your plan converts to month-to-month at the end of the annual term.

## Refunds on Annual Plans

If you cancel an annual plan before the term ends, you receive a pro-rated refund for unused full months. See [policy_refund.md](policy_refund.md) for the refund formula and process.

## Enterprise Annual Terms

Enterprise annual contracts are negotiated separately. Discounts, payment terms, and renewal processes are governed by the Master Service Agreement. Contact your Account Manager for details.
