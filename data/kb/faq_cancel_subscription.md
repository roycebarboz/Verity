# How Do I Cancel My Subscription?

**Document Type:** FAQ
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** All Plans
**Tier:** Tier 1

## Summary

You can cancel your CloudOps Platform subscription at any time through the Web Console. This article explains the cancellation process, what happens to your data afterward, and how refunds are handled.

## Steps to Cancel

1. Log in to [console.cloudops.example](https://console.cloudops.example).
2. Navigate to **Settings > Billing > Subscription**.
3. Click **Cancel Subscription** at the bottom of the page.
4. Select a cancellation reason from the dropdown (required for processing).
5. Review the summary of what will happen to your clusters and data.
6. Click **Confirm Cancellation**.

You will receive a confirmation email at your registered address within 5 minutes.

## What Happens After Cancellation

- **Your clusters remain accessible** until the end of your current billing period.
- After the billing period ends, all clusters are **stopped and put into a 30-day grace period**.
- During the grace period, you can reactivate your account and restore your clusters.
- After 30 days, cluster data is **permanently deleted** in accordance with our [Data Retention Policy](policy_data_retention.md).

## Refunds

Monthly plans: No refund for the current period — access continues until period end.
Annual plans: Pro-rated refund for unused full months. See [policy_refund.md](policy_refund.md) for details.

## Enterprise Plans

Enterprise cancellations must go through your dedicated Account Manager. Contact them directly or email `enterprise@cloudops.example`.
