# Legal and Compliance Escalation Criteria

**Document Type:** Escalation
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** All Plans
**Tier:** Tier 2

## Summary

This guide defines which customer inquiries and situations must be routed to the CloudOps Legal/Compliance team, bypassing standard Tier 1 and Tier 2 support resolution paths.

## Always Route to Legal/Compliance

Route a ticket immediately to Legal/Compliance (do not attempt to resolve at Tier 1 or Tier 2) for any of the following:

### Regulatory and Legal Requests
- Subpoenas, court orders, or law enforcement data requests
- GDPR data subject requests that go beyond the self-service portal capability (e.g., requests to stop all processing)
- CCPA or other privacy law compliance requests
- Requests related to export controls or sanctions compliance
- Requests from government agencies or regulators

### Intellectual Property
- Copyright infringement claims (DMCA takedown notices)
- Patent infringement allegations
- Trade secret disputes

### Contractual Disputes
- Customers disputing Master Service Agreement (MSA) terms
- Reseller contract disputes
- Enterprise SLA credit disputes that have been rejected by Tier 2 and the customer is threatening legal action

### Serious Security and Trust Events
- Reports of CSAM hosted on CloudOps infrastructure (report to authorities simultaneously)
- Reports of infrastructure being used to facilitate illegal activity
- Requests for law enforcement cooperation

## How to Route to Legal/Compliance

1. Do **not** reply to the customer with any commitments or substantive information.
2. Acknowledge receipt with a generic hold response: *"We have received your inquiry and are routing it to the appropriate team. You will receive a follow-up within 2 business days."*
3. In the support portal, escalate the ticket and select **Legal/Compliance** as the escalation type.
4. Send an email to `legal@cloudops.example` with the ticket ID in the subject line.
5. For urgent situations (active law enforcement request, imminent legal deadline), also call the Legal on-call line at the number provided in the internal support handbook.

## What NOT to Say to Customers

When routing to Legal/Compliance, avoid:
- Confirming or denying whether customer data exists or will be provided to law enforcement
- Making any commitment about timelines
- Discussing the specifics of contractual terms
- Expressing any opinion about the validity of the request
