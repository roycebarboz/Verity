# Beta Feature Terms

**Document Type:** Policy
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** All Plans
**Tier:** Tier 1

## Summary

CloudOps Platform periodically offers Beta features to customers for early access testing. This document describes the terms, limitations, and expectations for participating in Beta programs.

## What "Beta" Means

Beta features are features that are fully implemented and functional but are:
- Not yet covered by the standard SLA ([policy_sla.md](policy_sla.md))
- Subject to breaking changes or removal without notice
- Not supported by Tier 1 or Tier 2 support (Beta features are community and engineering-supported only)
- Potentially limited in availability (regional or plan-tier gated)

## Opting In

Beta features are opt-in. To enroll in an available Beta:
1. Log in to [console.cloudops.example](https://console.cloudops.example).
2. Navigate to **Settings > Beta Features**.
3. Toggle on the features you wish to try.
4. Read and accept the Beta Terms confirmation.

## What You Accept by Opting In

By enabling a Beta feature, you acknowledge:
- The feature may be modified or discontinued at any time without a refund or service credit.
- CloudOps is not liable for data loss, downtime, or other adverse effects caused by Beta features.
- Feedback you provide about Beta features may be used to improve the product.
- The Beta feature is provided "as-is" without warranty.

## Support for Beta Features

- **Tier 1 support** will acknowledge Beta-related tickets but cannot guarantee resolution timelines.
- Beta issues are routed to the Engineering team responsible for the feature.
- Known Beta issues are tracked in the [Beta Release Notes](https://console.cloudops.example/docs/beta-release-notes).

## Graduating to General Availability

When a Beta feature reaches General Availability (GA):
- It becomes covered by the standard SLA.
- Full Tier 1 and Tier 2 support applies.
- Breaking changes require a 30-day deprecation notice.
