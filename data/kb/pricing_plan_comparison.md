# Plan Comparison

**Document Type:** Pricing
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** All Plans
**Tier:** Tier 1

## Summary

This document provides a side-by-side comparison of all CloudOps Platform subscription plans, including features, limits, and pricing. Use this when answering customer questions about which plan is right for them.

## Plan Overview

| Feature | Starter | Professional | Enterprise |

| **Price** | $99/month | $499/month | Custom pricing |
| **Clusters** | Up to 3 | Up to 20 | Unlimited |
| **Node pools per cluster** | Up to 3 | Up to 10 | Unlimited |
| **Nodes per pool** | Up to 5 | Up to 50 | Negotiated |
| **Team members** | Up to 5 | Up to 25 | Unlimited |
| **API rate limit** | 60 req/min | 300 req/min | 1,000 req/min |
| **Uptime SLA** | 99.5% | 99.9% | 99.95% |
| **Support** | Email only | Priority email + chat | Dedicated CSM + phone |
| **P1 response time** | 8 business hours | 2 hours | 30 minutes |

| **Slack integration** | Not included | ✅ | ✅ |
| **PagerDuty integration** | Not included | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Hardware security keys** | Not included | Not included | ✅ |

## Annual vs Monthly Billing

Annual plans are billed upfront for 12 months and receive a **10% discount** compared to monthly billing:
- Starter Annual: $99 × 12 × 0.90 = **$1,069/year** ($89.10/month effective)
- Professional Annual: $499 × 12 × 0.90 = **$5,389/year** ($449.10/month effective)

See [pricing_annual_discount.md](pricing_annual_discount.md) for terms.
