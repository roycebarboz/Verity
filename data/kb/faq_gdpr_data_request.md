# How Do I Submit a GDPR Data Request?

**Document Type:** FAQ
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** All Plans
**Tier:** Tier 1

## Summary

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have rights over personal data we hold about you. This article explains how to submit a data subject access request (DSAR), request deletion, or request rectification of your data.

## Your Rights Under GDPR

- **Right of Access:** Receive a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"):** Request deletion of your personal data.
- **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
- **Right to Portability:** Receive your data in a machine-readable format.
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** Limit how we use your data.

## How to Submit a GDPR Request

**Option 1 — Self-service (Access and Deletion):**
1. Log in to [console.cloudops.example](https://console.cloudops.example).
2. Navigate to **Settings > Account > Privacy**.
3. Select the request type and click **Submit Request**.

**Option 2 — Email:**
Send your request to `privacy@cloudops.example` with the subject line: `GDPR Request — [Your Request Type]`. Include your registered email address and account ID.

## Response Timeline

We are required to respond within **30 days** of receiving a verifiable request. Complex requests may be extended to 60 days with notice.

## Verification

For security, we will verify your identity before processing any request. Identity verification requires matching your registered email address and may include additional confirmation steps.
