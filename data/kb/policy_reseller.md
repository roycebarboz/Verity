# Reseller Policy

**Document Type:** Policy
**Last Updated:** 2026-03-15
**Applies To:** Enterprise
**Tier:** Tier 2

## Summary

CloudOps Inc. operates a Reseller Program that allows authorized partners to resell CloudOps Platform subscriptions to their end customers. This document describes eligibility, responsibilities, and terms for CloudOps Resellers.

## Becoming an Authorized Reseller

To apply for the CloudOps Reseller Program:
1. Submit a reseller application at [cloudops.example/partners/reseller](https://cloudops.example/partners/reseller).
2. CloudOps will review the application within 10 business days.
3. Approved resellers receive a Reseller Agreement and access to the Partner Portal at [partners.cloudops.example](https://partners.cloudops.example).

## Reseller Responsibilities

Authorized resellers are responsible for:
- Accurately representing CloudOps Platform features, pricing, and terms to end customers
- Collecting payments from end customers and remitting agreed fees to CloudOps
- Providing first-line support to their end customers (Tier 1 equivalent)
- Not making modifications to CloudOps software or documentation
- Ensuring end customers comply with the CloudOps Acceptable Use Policy

## What Resellers May Not Do

- Resell CloudOps services at prices that misrepresent the product's value
- Bundle CloudOps with services that violate the Acceptable Use Policy
- Sub-license or transfer the reseller agreement without written approval
- Represent themselves as CloudOps employees or agents

## End Customer Accounts

End customers provisioned by a reseller are managed through the Partner Portal. The reseller holds the primary contractual relationship with CloudOps; the end customer's agreement is with the reseller.

CloudOps may, at its discretion, require direct agreements with end customers for Enterprise accounts over certain revenue thresholds.

## Support Escalation for Reseller Accounts

Resellers must handle Tier 1 support independently. For Tier 2 and above escalations, resellers submit tickets through the Partner Portal on behalf of their end customers.

## Termination

Either party may terminate the reseller agreement with 30 days written notice. Upon termination, resellers must transition active end customer accounts to direct CloudOps accounts within 60 days.
